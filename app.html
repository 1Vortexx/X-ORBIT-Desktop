Privacy from Monitoring Software
School monitoring tools like GoGuardian, Securly, and Hapara operate within Chrome tabs, allowing them to view activity, track URLs, and block content — but only inside the browser. When X-ORBIT runs as an installed app (PWA), it operates as a separate process outside the tab system, similar to standalone applications. Because of this separation, monitoring extensions cannot access or view activity within it. While the system may detect that the app was opened, it cannot see what occurs inside.

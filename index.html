What's inside

Built around what you actually use.

Everything runs in your browser — no installs, no extensions, no configuration.

Proxy Browser
Browse freely using Orbix — our own engine built on Ultraviolet — or Rammerhead, a third-party backend we integrate for extra coverage. Both route through X-ORBIT's servers.
Spotify Music
Listen to Spotify without leaving your environment. Music streams through X-ORBIT's proxied nginx servers via the Spotify SDK.
Games
Minecraft (Eaglercraft) is built in, with more games being added based on community requests. No separate launcher needed.
Direct Messaging
Message other X-ORBIT users directly. Share text and — on Premium — images, all within your account.
Secure Accounts
Your account stores your preferences and keeps your data yours. Required to prevent abuse and keep X-ORBIT available for everyone.
Desktop Environment
A fully functional OS-style environment — apps, a menubar, and window management — running entirely inside a browser tab.
Proxy engines

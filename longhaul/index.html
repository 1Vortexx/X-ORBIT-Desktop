STAYING ALIVE
  • Hunger, thirst and energy drop over time. If one runs out, your health starts falling. Thirst drops faster in the midday heat.
  • The truck wears out: tyres (faster off-road), the engine (faster when it runs hot or in sandstorms), coolant, and the battery if you leave the lights on with the engine off.
  • Watch the temperature gauge. Push a hot engine with low coolant and it will damage itself, then cut out.
  • Crashing into rocks, poles and wrecks damages the truck and you.
  • Rain makes the road slippery. Sandstorms cut visibility to almost nothing.
  • Abandoned cars can be searched once for cash and supplies. Station shops sell food, water and parts. Fuel costs $1.60 a litre.

Announcement May 2026 X-ORBIT Team

End of Year Shutdown — We'll Be Back in August

As the 2025–26 school year wraps up, we are temporarily shutting down X-ORBIT services until August 2026. Here's everything you need to know.

Services are currently offline
X-ORBIT is shut down as of May 2026. All services will return in August 2026 for the 2026–27 school year. Your account and data are safe and unaffected.

Why we shut down every year

X-ORBIT was built for students, and demand for our services follows the school calendar closely. As the academic year comes to an end, usage drops significantly — most of our users are finishing up exams, heading home for the summer, or simply offline for the break.

Rather than paying to keep our infrastructure running at full capacity for a fraction of normal traffic, we shut down each summer to cut costs. This is something we do every year, and it lets us reinvest those savings into improvements for the next school year.

This is not an emergency, a security incident, or anything negative — it is a planned, routine part of how we operate.

Timeline

2025–26 School Year
X-ORBIT was fully operational throughout the 2025–26 academic year, serving students across the network.
Now — Shutdown (May–July 2026)
All X-ORBIT services are offline for the summer. Logins are disabled. Your account remains intact and will be waiting when we return.
August 2026 — We Return
Services will be restored ahead of the 2026–27 school year. Expect improvements, updates, and the same X-ORBIT experience you're used to.

A note from the team

Thank you to everyone who used X-ORBIT this year. We genuinely appreciate your support, and we hope the platform has been useful throughout the school year.

We know a summer break can be inconvenient, and we don't take that lightly. But keeping costs manageable is what allows us to offer X-ORBIT free of charge — and to keep improving it year after year.

We'll be back in August 2026, refreshed and ready for the 2026–27 school year. Until then, take care — and enjoy your summer.

— The X-ORBIT Team
